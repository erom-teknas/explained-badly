Explained, Badly

Explained, Badly is a channel of short, hand-drawn animated explainers. Watch it on YouTube.

The Explained Badly app

Explained Badly is also the name of the channel's own publishing tool. It posts the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts, including TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not offered to anyone else.

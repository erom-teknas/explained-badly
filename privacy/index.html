Explained, Badly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Explained Badly is a publishing tool used by the channel "Explained, Badly" to post its own videos to its own social media accounts.

What we collect

When the channel's owner signs in to TikTok through the tool, TikTok gives the tool an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID, username and display name. Nothing else is collected. The tool does not collect anything from viewers, followers or anyone else.

How it's used

Only to post the channel's own videos to the channel's own account, and to check that posting is going to the right account.

Where it's kept

Only on the one computer that runs the tool, in a file readable by the owner's computer account alone. It isn't sent to any other server, sold, or shared.

How long

Until the owner signs out or revokes access. To revoke: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, Security and permissions (or "Apps and websites"), and remove Explained Badly. Revoking makes the stored tokens useless; the file is deleted when the channel stops using the tool.
